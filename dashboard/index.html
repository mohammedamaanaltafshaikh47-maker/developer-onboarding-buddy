Turn any codebase into a guided path

New developers usually spend 2–4 weeks just figuring out how a codebase works. This tool reads the code, understands its structure and history, and automatically builds a day-by-day learning path — with diagrams, the most important files to read, and quizzes to check understanding.

How it works

01
Point it at any codebase It analyzes the project's file structure and commit history to understand which parts are most important and most frequently changed.
02
It builds a personalized path Architecture diagrams, key files ranked by importance, and a day-by-day plan tailored to how the codebase is actually structured.
03
The developer follows along Reading, exploring files, and short quizzes confirm real understanding — not just passive reading.
